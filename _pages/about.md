Hello! I am Jason Li, a Ph.D. student in Computing at the **University of Utah** (Data Management and Analysis track), advised by Professor **Bei Wang Phillips** in the Topological Intelligence Lab (TILab).

My research lies at the intersection of **explainable AI, topological data analysis, deep learning, and human-AI interaction**. Broadly, I am interested in making complex, high-dimensional data easier to explore, understand, and verify.

My work spans two connected directions:

- **Explaining learned representations**. Developing methods for visualizing and interpreting the latent representations of deep learning models, so that complex model behavior becomes more transparent and understandable.
- **Agentic frameworks for knowledge graph exploration**. Designing AI systems that explore knowledge graphs — discovering entities, relationships, and workflows across scientific literature and data — and that produce findings a user can check against the underlying evidence.
