Hello! I am **Jason Li**, a PhD student in Computing at the **University of Utah**, advised by Professor **Bei Wang Phillips** in the Topological Intelligence Lab (TILab).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **explainable AI, deep learning, data visualization, and human-AI interaction**. In particular, I am interested in developing methods for visualizing and interpreting the latent representations of deep learning models, with the goal of making complex model behavior more transparent and understandable.
